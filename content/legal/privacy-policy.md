## Who we are

This website is operated by {entity} (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it and how we look after it. We handle personal information in accordance with applicable data protection law, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## Information we collect

We collect only the information you choose to give us when you submit an inquiry or consultation request:

- your name, and your business or organization;
- your email address, phone number and country;
- the service you are interested in, your project description, approximate budget and preferred times;
- your preferred contact method.

When a form is submitted, our systems also process technical information such as your IP address for the limited purposes of preventing spam and abuse.

## How we use it

We use this information only to:

- respond to your inquiry or arrange a consultation;
- discuss and, where agreed, provide the services you have asked about;
- protect this website and its forms from spam and misuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

## Basis for processing

We process the information you submit on the basis of your consent, given when you submit the form, and to take steps at your request before entering into an agreement with you. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us.

## Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts and other assets are served from our own website. Our spam protection may use strictly necessary technical data to tell people apart from automated software. If we introduce analytics in future, this policy will be updated first.

## Sharing and storage

Your inquiry is delivered to our business mailbox and is accessible only to the people who need it to respond. We use reputable infrastructure providers to host this website and deliver messages; they process data on our behalf and under our instructions. We do not otherwise share your information unless required by law.

## How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information only as long as needed to respond and to manage any resulting engagement, after which it is deleted, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

## Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or erase the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by contacting us through {contact}. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable law.

## Security

This website is served over an encrypted connection. Form submissions are validated, protected against automated abuse and transmitted securely. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

We may update this policy from time to time; the date at the top shows when it last changed. Questions about this policy can be sent through {contact}.
